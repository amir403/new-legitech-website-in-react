AI & AUTOMATION / 4 MIN READ

Automate the right things.

A practical starting point for reducing repetitive work in your business.

IN THIS ARTICLE

Look for repetition and friction.

List the tasks that repeatedly move the same information between people or tools. Note the time involved, the error rate you observe and the decisions a person still needs to make.

Improve the process first.

Automating an unclear process can make confusion move faster. Agree on the trigger, the required information, the expected outcome and the person responsible for exceptions.

Choose simple rules when they are enough.

Not every automation needs AI. Predictable routing, reminders and data synchronisation often work well with explicit rules. AI may help with less structured information, but its output needs evaluation.

Keep a human review path.

Identify decisions that need approval, define what happens when confidence is low and make it possible to correct an output. Keep access to sensitive information limited to what the task requires.

Measure a small pilot.

Start with a contained workflow and compare the results with how the task works today. Review time saved, errors, exceptions and maintenance needs before expanding.
