Privacy notice

How the current website handles your project-brief information.

About this notice

This notice describes the current website and its project-brief form. It does not describe data processing in a future client project, which should be covered by its own agreed documentation.

Project-brief information

The form lets you enter your name, email, company and project details. The website does not send this information to LegiTech.io or save it to a website database. It remains in the page while it is open.

Copying and downloading

When you choose Copy project brief, the text is placed on your device clipboard. When you choose Download brief, a text file is saved to your device. You decide whether and how to share that information.

Website services

The page loads fonts from Google Fonts, which involves requests to Google’s servers. Hosting and access are provided by the website platform and may involve technical logs or authentication managed by that provider. This website code does not add analytics, advertising trackers or browser storage.

Your choices

You can leave the form blank, close the page without sharing, or clear your clipboard and delete downloaded briefs. Avoid including sensitive information in a project brief.
